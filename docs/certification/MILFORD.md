### Municipal tax, sewer and utility sources rechecked 2026-10-27

- **FY2027 Milford municipal base mill rate: 28.67.** The Connecticut Conference of Municipalities data hub lists the FY2027 real/personal property mill rate as **28.67**: https://hub.ccm-ct.org/community/milford/ . This verifies the municipal base, **not** any parcel's total including applicable special districts, credits, or separate fees. Special-district rate records still require independent source review.
- **Five-year revaluation phase-in.** Milford's official Assessor page explains the October 1, 2025 revaluation, adopted five-year phase-in, annual assessment notices, and why the new full assessed value can differ from the taxable phased-in amount: https://milfordct.us/210/Assessors-Office . The calculator's taxable-assessment override is therefore important; buyers must consult the current bill.
- **Sewer user fee.** Milford's Sewer Commission explicitly lists **$361.92 per unit for the 2025 Grand List** and **$346.44 for the 2024 Grand List**: https://www.milfordct.us/744/Sewer-Commission . The Tax Collector confirms sewer fees are collected separately from real estate tax and may appear as a separate line on a mailed bill: https://www.milfordct.us/663/Tax-Collector . This supports the dated fee already displayed; it does not establish the charge for every parcel.
- **Regional Water Authority.** Its official start/stop page lists Milford among served communities and gives moving/closing instructions: https://www.rwater.com/customer-care/start-or-stop-service/ . Do not infer that every Milford address has an RWA connection.
- **Southern Connecticut Gas.** Its service-area page lists Milford under New Haven County and directs users to check street-level availability: https://www.soconngas.com/ourcompany/whoweare/servicearea . Do not promise gas service to a particular address.
